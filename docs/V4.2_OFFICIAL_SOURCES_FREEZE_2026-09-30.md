# B3 Architecture V4.2 — Official Sources Freeze — 2026-09-30

**Status:** FROZEN / PRODUCTION VALIDATED  
**Scope:** CVM official-source acquisition, issuer identity, canonical Evidence and deterministic official materiality.  
**Integration target:** `fix/react-portfolio-api`

## Frozen decisions

V4.2 freezes the following contracts:

- CVM Open Data is the public historical/backfill/reconciliation lane.
- CVM Download Múltiplo/RAD is the authenticated live-observation lane.
- issuer and ticker identity are resolved deterministically through the Issuer Registry; no LLM identity inference is allowed.
- all official observations normalize into the existing canonical `Evidence` model.
- `Data_Referencia` and delivery/observation time remain distinct.
- Open Data reconstruction uses `HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION`.
- Download Múltiplo live observations use `OBSERVED_LIVE`.
- deterministic official materiality executes before any LLM.
- official source authority is `OFFICIAL_REGULATORY`, authority tier 0.
- acquisition and Evidence persistence remain independent from local/senior LLM quality.

V4.3 may change the role and scheduling of local derived reasoning, but does not reopen these V4.2 authority contracts.

## Freeze rule

The following require a new architecture version or explicit amendment rather than silent mutation:

- official-source authority hierarchy;
- issuer/ticker deterministic identity contract;
- canonical Evidence semantics;
- PIT distinction between live observation and historical reconstruction;
- deterministic materiality-before-LLM rule.

Implementation defects may be fixed without reopening the architecture as long as these invariants remain unchanged.
