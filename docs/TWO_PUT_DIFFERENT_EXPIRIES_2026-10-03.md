# Two PUT sales with different expiries — 2026-10-03

Extends the existing two-alternative Strategy Lab flow (choose Vender PUT on both sides and exact current OPLAB contracts). Different underlyings and expiries are supported by that canonical comparator; the new payload/table exposes per-contract expiry/duration, bid/ask, volume/OI, provider multiplier, premium, collateral, break-even, maximum pre-cost loss, gross premium/capital and simple 30-day normalization. Each expiry has its own uncalibrated ITM/touch lognormal proxy. Exercise style and unknown early assignment are separate. Net expected return, fees/taxes and personal exercise frequency remain UNKNOWN.

Default is no winner. Two optional objective-specific conditional orders are lower model expiry-ITM or higher gross premium per capital normalized to 30 days. These are not a combined risk-adjusted score or economic recommendation. Different expiry probabilities cover different exposure periods; 30-day normalization is not expected return or repeated achievable reinvestment. Missing IV blocks risk ranking. Common terminal scenarios that do not cover both exact expiries cannot select a maximin winner.

The pair uses existing providers through the strict AssetEvidencePack screen and existing StrategyComparison, no new ledger or execution. Chain acquisition completes before one frozen live cutoff. Exact contract/quote identity and uniqueness, availability/observation cutoff, source/quality, positive finite strike/multiplier/bid and maximum quote age seven calendar days are enforced. Historical pair replay is refused because historical contract availability is not established. UI performs no finance calculations.
