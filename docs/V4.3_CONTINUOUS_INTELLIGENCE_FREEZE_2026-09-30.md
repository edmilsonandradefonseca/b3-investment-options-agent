## 6. Frozen invariants

V4.3 freezes these decisions:

- canonical Evidence is authoritative; local dossiers are derived/rebuildable;
- DeepSeek is asynchronous and never mandatory before OpenClaw/Luna;
- deterministic materiality precedes every LLM;
- official MATERIAL cannot be suppressed by local relevance;
- only READY, current, reference-valid dossiers may enrich senior context;
- DEGRADED, DEFERRED, FAILED, stale or mismatched dossiers are omitted;
- runtime local-model failures are deferred/requeued rather than losing Evidence;
- discovery is restart-safe and cursor advancement follows successful persistence;
- CVM live observation remains distinct from Open Data historical reconstruction;
- B3 and João share host-level heavy-inference arbitration;
- issuer/security identity is deterministic and FCA trading-code constrained;
- production APIs expose read-only Continuous Intelligence observability;
- no autonomous trading authority is introduced.

Do not silently mutate V4.3 after this freeze.
